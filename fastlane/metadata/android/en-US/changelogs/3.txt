Offline regex-crossword puzzles for Android.

* Easy (rectangle 5x5) and Medium / Hard (hexagon) difficulties.
* Puzzles generated on device from a seed; Hard levels pre-generated with the
  solver so their answer is unique.
* Notes, per-cell hint, givens, undo/redo and a per-level timer.
* Progress saved automatically. No accounts, ads, network access or permissions.
* Release APK keeps R8 shrinking (smaller download) and is reproducible, so
  F-Droid ships the developer's own signature.
